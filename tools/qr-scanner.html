⚙️How it works

Uses the html5-qrcode engine to scan your camera feed at ~10fps inside a framed scan box (pick a rear camera from the list when available), or decodes a QR/barcode from an uploaded image via scanFile. Whatever is detected — QR code, Data Matrix, barcode formats — is shown with its format and time.

🔒Privacy

Camera frames and uploaded images are decoded entirely on-device — nothing leaves your browser.

💡Tips
  • Camera access needs HTTPS or localhost and a permission prompt — if denied, allow it in browser settings and reload, or use the image upload method.
  • Plain even lighting and a flat, close-up angle give the fastest reads.
  • Click Stop camera after detection to free the camera for other apps.
