Currency Converter

Convert between 40+ world currencies using live daily exchange rates. Fast, free, and private.

Business client-side

⚙️How it works

On conversion, the tool fetches a daily snapshot of mid-market rates (every supported currency measured against the USD base) from exchangerate-api.com, then computes your exact pair with a cross-rate: target ÷ source. The latest snapshot is cached in this device's localStorage, so already-seen pairs keep converting even offline. The date shown is when that rate snapshot was last updated.

🔒Privacy

The lookup is anonymous — no account, no key. Your amount never leaves the page; only the currency symbols needed for the rate table are shared with the rate provider.

💡Tips
  • Rates are indicative mid-market (daily); banks and card networks typically add their own margin on top.
  • Press Enter in the amount field or use the ⇄ swap button to convert without clicking Convert.
  • Copy the result line anywhere — paste it into a doc or send it in a message.
