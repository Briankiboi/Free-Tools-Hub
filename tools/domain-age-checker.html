Domain Age Checker

Finds the creation date & age of a domain using the authoritative Verisign RDAP registry, plus live DNS records.

Domains & DNS client-side

Result

Enter a domain (e.g. example.com) and click Check. Age + DNS answers load from Verisign RDAP & Google Public DNS.

⚙️How it works

Looks up the domain's registry record through RDAP (the modern WHOIS) — rdap.org first, with rdap.verisign.com for .com/.net and rdap.registry.net as fallbacks. This returns the authoritative created, updated and expiry dates plus registrar and nameservers. The tool then makes a second pass over Google Public DNS for common records (A, MX, NS, TXT…) and calculates the domain's age in years from its registry creation date.

🔒Privacy

Everything is fetched live from public registries and DNS — only the domain name you type is sent, and nothing is stored locally or tracked.

💡Tips
  • Age is the registry's created date — the most reliable indicator of a domain's true age.
  • A brand-new domain may show few or no DNS records until its owner configures them.
  • If contact fields look missing, that's registrar GDPR redaction — not a query error.
